`LoRALinear.__init__`: `self.base = base` (no copy) and `p.requires_grad = False` for its parameters. `self.lora_A = nn.Parameter(torch.empty(r, base.in_features))` initialised with `nn.init.kaiming_uniform_(self.lora_A, a=math.sqrt(5), generator=generator)`, `self.lora_B = nn.Parameter(torch.zeros(base.out_features, r))`, `self.scale = alpha / r`. `forward`: `self.base(x) + self.scale * ((x @ self.lora_A.T) @ self.lora_B.T)`.

Skip modules that are themselves a `LoRALinear`. Raise `ValueError` if the list is empty.
