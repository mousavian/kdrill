`dpo_loss`: `margin = beta * ((pc - rc.detach()) - (pr - rr.detach()))` and `return -F.logsigmoid(margin).mean()`. `implicit_rewards` returns `beta * (pc - rc), beta * (pr - rr)`. Check by hand: all four inputs equal gives `margin = 0` and a loss of `ln 2`.
---
`reference_logprobs`: build the chosen items `(prompt_ids, chosen_ids)` and rejected items `(prompt_ids, rejected_ids)` with `encode_pair` for every pair; then `torch.cat([completion_logprobs(model, items[i:i + batch_size]) for i in range(0, len(items), batch_size)])` for each list, under `@torch.no_grad()` with the model in eval mode (restore `model.training`). `win_rate`: the same four tensors for policy and reference, then `float((((pc - rc) - (pr - rr)) > 0).float().mean())`.
---
`dpo_train`: encode all pairs once; per step `ix = torch.randint(0, len(pairs), (batch_size,), generator=g).tolist()`, policy `pc`, `pr` for those items with `completion_logprobs`, loss from `dpo_loss(pc, pr, rc[ix], rr[ix], beta)`, then `opt.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`, `loss.backward()`, `opt.step()` with AdamW (`weight_decay=0.0`). If the first loss is not 0.693, the policy and the reference differ (did you train the reference, or compute `rc`, `rr` with a different `pad_id` or mode?). If the chatty share does not fall, lower the batch noise by checking the sign of the margin: chosen minus rejected, not the reverse.
